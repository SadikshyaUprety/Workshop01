About Us

Welcome to Sandesh and Sadikshya Kirana Store, your trusted local destination in Kavrepalanchok for all your everyday needs. We proudly offer a wide variety of high-quality products, including fresh daily dairy items like milk and curd, essential groceries, and cosmetic goods, all at affordable prices. Conveniently located with easy transportation access, we are dedicated to providing fresh items, excellent quality, and friendly service to ensure complete customer satisfaction.

Products

At Sandesh and Sadikshya Kirana Store, we stock a comprehensive range of daily essentials to meet every household need. Our Grocery section offers fresh grains, pulses, cooking oils, spices, and packaged foods. In our Dairy section, you will find farm-fresh milk, curd (dahi), and ghee supplied daily. We also provide a great selection of Cosmetics and Personal Care products, including soaps, lotions, shampoos, and everyday beauty items—all carefully chosen for quality and affordability.
